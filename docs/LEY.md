# La Ley — index of the governing documents

> **For humans.** The map of every document that governs this project, grouped by the
> function each one performs in the system.
>
> **Epistemic value.** Resolves "which document rules on X?" in one lookup — the index of
> the project's entire normative model.
> **Pragmatic value.** Any new law document must register here with its charter; any agent
> starting a task walks this list before assuming anything.
> **In the system.** Observes: every chartered .md. Regulates: where authority lives.
> Coupled to: everything below.

Every law document opens with a **charter**: what it is (for humans, always on top), its
**epistemic value** (what uncertainty it resolves — what you learn), its **pragmatic
value** (what actions it enables or constrains), and its place **in the system** (what it
observes, what it regulates, what it couples to). The frame is systems thinking + active
inference: documents are how this organism keeps its world-model and its actions aligned.
