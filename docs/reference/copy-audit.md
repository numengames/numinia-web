# Copy Audit — Numinia store (ES/EN)

**Verdict.** The copy is remarkably consistent in voice (tú throughout ES, personified-city register, spaced em dashes) and EN/ES pairs are mostly faithful. The real problems are: (1) internal engineering vocabulary leaking into citizen-facing strings (Fase 3/5, D23, ADR, v0.1.0); (2) terminology drift on core nouns — _wallet/cartera_, _Actualizaciones/Novedades_, _Arconte/Archonte_, _Ágora Plaza/Plaza del Ágora_; (3) the L.A.P. acronym is never expanded anywhere; (4) one likely canon typo with comic effect ("Conejos Legales" = "Legal Rabbits"); (5) a handful of EN/ES pairs that don't say the same thing (`settings.languageNote`, `typeLabels.UPD`). Files: all under `/var/home/pablofm/Documentos/Repositorios/numinia-platform/apps/store/src/`.

## Terminology drift pairs

1. **wallet ⇄ cartera** (ES): `lap.ts` census.intro "una wallet", FIELDS_ES `wallet: 'Wallet'` vs `lap.ts` admin.forbiddenNote "la cartera", `settings.ts` sessionIntro "tu propia cartera".
2. **Actualizaciones ⇄ Novedades** (ES): `messages.ts` navUpdates/updatesTitle vs `lap.ts` sections.updates.
3. **Arconte ⇄ Archonte** (ES): `city-landing.ts:287` vs `lap.ts:247`.
4. **Plaza del Ágora ⇄ Ágora Plaza** (ES): `lap.ts:300` vs `city-landing.ts:226`.
5. **Manual ⇄ Códice** (ES/EN): `lap.ts` manual.gated\* says the _Codex_ opens for the _Manual_ gate; sections.codex is a different thing.
6. **Recursos (docs) ⇄ Documentación ⇄ Recursos (footer group)**: `messages.ts` navDocs/docsTitle vs sidebarLabel vs footerResources.
7. **Assets ⇄ bienes digitales** (ES): nav/pillars "Assets" vs archiveIntro/legal "bienes digitales" (arguably deliberate register split; worth a style-guide line).
8. **Guild-tree EN names**: "Craftsmen/Designers" (`city-landing.ts`) vs canon "Artisans/Projectors" (CLAUDE.md/domain); ES branches "Legados" vs canon "Juristas"; houses "Mercuriales, Intendentes" vs canon "Tesoreros, Concejales"; plus "Conejos/Consejos Legales".
9. **Nómada ⇄ nómada** capitalization (ES) across `city-landing.ts`, `lap.ts`, `settings.ts`; EN consistently "Nomad".
10. **Diurno/Nocturno** untranslated in EN (`settings.ts`) vs "Color mode / Modo de color" plain terms in chrome (`messages.ts` modeToggle).
